## 1. Forum post (thread "Promote your game here")

**Title:**
EarthVault – free browser quiz games played on real, live planet data (no login, no ads)

**Body:**

Hi all! I made **EarthVault**, a free web app where the whole planet is the game board. Everything runs on open data, nothing is made up, and most of it is live.

🎮 **The games**
- **Planet Quiz** – seven kinds of question built from real numbers: higher or lower between two countries, pick the top of four, capitals, flags, estimate on a slider, which capital is warmer *right now*, and where it is later in the day. Streaks, confetti, and a global leaderboard where every round counts.
- **Hot or Cold** – a live temperature duel between two cities.
- **Flag Flash** – flags against the clock.
- **Mystery Country** – one daily country to guess from its data, Wordle style.
- **Challenge a friend** – send a link and your friend plays the exact same questions you got. Then compare scores.

🌍 **Around the games**
- A **3D live globe** with fires, storms, quakes, volcanoes, the temperature of every capital and the ISS moving in real time.
- **Country Twins** – type a country and find the five countries most like it across 25 measures.
- **The Planet Since You Were Born** – your birth date in, your personal climate report out.
- **Earth Stories** – guided tours through the data, including one rewritten from the live feeds every time it opens.
- 47 data "vaults" in total: weather history for any city, oceans, forests, energy, air, happiness, and more.

It is a single web page, installs as an app on Android, and works in English and Greek.

▶ **Play:** https://chamitro.github.io/EarthVault/
🎯 Jump straight to the quiz: https://chamitro.github.io/EarthVault/?vault=quiz

Data from the World Bank, NASA, NOAA, USGS, GDACS, Open-Meteo and Our World in Data. Built by one person, free, no account needed. I would love feedback on the games, especially which question kinds you find too easy or too hard.

## 2. Project page description (if you create an EarthVault page on itch.io)

**Tagline (short description field, max ~140 characters):**
Quiz games and a live 3D globe built on real planet data. Flags, capitals, climate, the ISS. Free, no login, English and Greek.

**Description:**

**EarthVault turns the planet into a game.**

Every question, every chart and every dot on the globe comes from real open data: the World Bank, NASA, NOAA, USGS, GDACS, Open-Meteo and Our World in Data. Much of it is live. Ask which capital is warmer right now and the answer is the temperature at this minute.

**Play**

- **Planet Quiz** – seven kinds of question mixed at random. Higher or lower between two countries on 25 measures, top of four, capitals, flags, an estimate slider, warmer right now, and local time. Build a streak, hit confetti milestones, and post your run to the global leaderboard, where every round counts.
- **Hot or Cold** – guess which of two cities is warmer at this moment.
- **Flag Flash** – name the flag before the timer runs out.
- **Mystery Country** – a new country every day; guess it from its numbers in as few tries as you can.
- **Challenge a friend** – one link, the same questions for both of you, a shared result page.

**Explore**

- **Live Globe** – a 3D Earth you spin and zoom, with live wildfires, cyclones, floods, volcanoes, a week of earthquakes, every capital coloured by its temperature and the International Space Station crossing the sky every 92 minutes. Colour the countries by forest cover, life expectancy, happiness or emissions, and tap any country to open its profile.
- **Country Profile** – one page per country: 25 measures with their world rank, the weather in the capital, active alerts and the five countries that look most like it.
- **Country Twins** – Greece's closest twin is Italy at 91%. Find yours.
- **The Planet Since You Were Born** – CO₂, temperature, forests and population on your birthday versus today, plus the weather in the city you were born in on that day.
- **Earth Stories** – scroll-driven tours drawn chart by chart: the carbon century, where the forests went, eight billion of us, the heating Mediterranean, and "This week on Earth", rebuilt from the live feeds on every visit.
- **Perfect Weather Finder**, **Planet Alerts**, **Best Hour Outside**, **Space Weather**, 50 years of weather history for any city, and more: 47 vaults in all.

**Details**

- Runs in any modern browser, no download, no account, no ads.
- Installs as an app on Android and desktop Chrome.
- English and Greek, switch with one tap.
- Phone-first layout; works just as well on a laptop.
- Open source on GitHub: https://github.com/chamitro/EarthVault

Made by one person in Greece. Feedback and bug reports are very welcome.

**Suggested tags:** educational, quiz, trivia, geography, browser, html5, data, science, earth, singleplayer, free

**Suggested itch.io settings:** Kind of project "HTML" is for files you upload; EarthVault is hosted on GitHub Pages, so pick "Other" or "Downloadable" with no files and put the play link in the description, or upload the repository as an HTML project with index.html as the entry file (it works inside the itch.io frame because all data comes from public APIs). Classification "Games", genre "Educational" or "Puzzle".
